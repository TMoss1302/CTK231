Games

Game Demos

A couple of games I am currently working on in Unity. The Long Drive: A driving game where the objective is to stay on the road and reach your destination. Gas and road conditions are important, but you must reach your destination no matter how crazy things get. Dead Shop: A zombie game where you play as Patient Zero, lead your horde, and kill as many survivors as you can to grow your numbers before more reinforcements arrive. These games are in their very early stages, and I am working on them alone. I cannot wait to show you what I have in store.
